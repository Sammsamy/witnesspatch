## Facilitator integrity check

- [ ] No participant answer was prefilled or paraphrased into agreement.
- [ ] No real patient data, patient question, proprietary incident, credential, or unauthorized target was used.
- [ ] The original result remains preserved even if a rerun or revision followed.
- [ ] Environment, all assistance, failures, objections, conflicts, and disagreements are included.
- [ ] Historical workflow time and observed clean-run time are not presented as equivalent tasks.
- [ ] No `n=1` percentage, adoption, time-saved, clinical-safety, or patient-outcome claim was created.
- [ ] The participant approved every public identity, credential, organization, or quote field used.
